### 1. Check Eligibility

Visit the airdrop site and paste your wallet address. You’ll instantly know whether you qualify.

- ✅ If you're eligible, you’ll see the amount you can claim.
- ❌ If not, you can still participate by buying and staking $NATION.

### 2. Connect Your Wallet

Click “Connect Wallet” to link the address you searched. Make sure it matches the eligible address shown on screen.

> Note: You must claim using the same wallet address that interacted with Nation.fun.
If you signed in using social login, follow this guide to back up and access your wallet:
[How to Back Up Your Wallet](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HDS0nhvTobMi).

### 3. Accept the Terms

Before claiming, you must accept the platform’s terms. This includes understanding that:

- You retain full control of your assets.
- Your engagement is at your own discretion.
- You are responsible for your wallet and private keys.

### 4. Choose Your Claim Method

Once you accept the terms, you’ll be given two options:

### Option 1: Claim Only

- You receive the base airdrop amount immediately

### Option 2: Claim + Stake (Recommended)

- You receive the base airdrop amount which you can stake for more rewards
- Maximum staking period is 356 days
- You can unstake anytime to claim your accumulated rewards
- The Staking pool has limited capacity. First come first serve
- You start earning 600% APY rewards on top of your base rewards

⚠️ Staked tokens have a 7-day unbonding period before they can be withdrawn.

### 5. Confirm Your Choice

After selecting your preferred option, confirm the transaction in your wallet. Once confirmed:

- Claimed tokens will appear in your wallet.
- Staked tokens and rewards will be visible on your staking dashboard.
