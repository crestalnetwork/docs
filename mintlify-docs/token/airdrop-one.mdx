### 🧾 Core Requirements

Each eligible wallet had to meet **both** of the following criteria:

- ✅ At least **2 linked social accounts**\
  (e.g., Twitter, Discord, Telegram, Lens, Farcaster)
- ✅ At least **2 social follow actions**\
  (e.g., followed @crestalnetwork on Twitter, joined Discord, etc.)

---

### 🧱 Activity-Based Participation

To reflect real engagement, users needed to show meaningful activity in at least one of the following:

- **Season 1:** Minimum **10 Rock points**
- **Season 2:** Minimum **20,000 Rock points**
- **Season 3:** No minimum, but **boosters** applied for topping up **CAPs**

📌 **Note:** Not all seasons were weighted equally in the reward formula:

- **Season 3** = Highest weight
- **Season 1** = Medium weight
- **Season 2** = Lowest weight

---

### 💥 Reward Boost Multipliers

Eligible users could **boost their allocation** using the following:

- **NFT Holdings**\
  🔹 Up to **2×** multiplier\
  🔹 Scales linearly up to 50 NFTs
- **No Hands Token Holdings**\
  🔹 Up to **1.5×** multiplier\
  🔹 Scales based on % of total supply held
- **Discord Roles**\
  🔹 \+0.1× per role\
  🔹 Applies across five roles: **VIP**, **Hector Salamanca**, **Stay Hydrated**, **Skinny Pete**, **Founder**\
  🔹 Max role multiplier: **1.5×**
- **Linked Social Accounts**\
  🔹 Bonus multiplier applied based on number of different verified platforms connected\
  🔹 Starts at 1.0× for 2 socials (minimum eligibility)\
  🔹 Increases by \+0.1× per additional social\
  🔹 Caps at 1.5× for 7 linked socials

---

### 🧑‍⚖️ Fairness & Sybil Protection

We applied filters and nerfs to ensure fairness:

- ❌ Wallets with **fewer than 2 linked socials or follows** were excluded
- ❌ Wallets with **duplicate social accounts** or bot-like behavior were removed
- ⚠️ **Referral abuse was nerfed** — wallets that gained points only through mass referrals without completing any quests (e.g. 6,000\+ referrals) received significantly reduced rewards

---

### 📊 Universal Ranking & Cutoff

All wallets were ranked on a **global leaderboard** using:

- Combined Rock scores across all seasons
- Adjusted by boost multipliers and season weights

🛑 **Only the top 19,154 wallets received allocations**\
Wallets ranked beyond this cutoff received **0 tokens**, to ensure rewards remain meaningful.

---

### 🎁 Special Reward Pools

In addition to the main airdrop:

- **IntentKit Developers** received bonus rewards for building on the protocol
- **VIPs** were rewarded for long-term contribution and community support
