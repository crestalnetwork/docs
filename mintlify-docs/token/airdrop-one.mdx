### Who Is Eligible

You may be eligible if you:

- Participated in Season 1, 2, or 3 by completing quests and earning Rocks
- Linked at least two social accounts (e.g. Twitter, Telegram, Discord)
- Followed Nation on at least two platforms (Twitter, Telegram, Discord)

### How We Calculate Rewards

Your airdrop amount depends on both your activity and your engagement level:

- The more Rocks you earned, the higher your base allocation
- Multipliers are applied for:
    - Holding Nation NFTs
    - Linking multiple social accounts
    - Being a top holder of the “No Hands” token

Each season is rewarded separately, and allocations are proportional to your contribution within each.
